# ggpalettes 0.2.0

- Expanded the catalogue to 26 original categorical, sequential, and
  diverging palettes.
- Added `pal_*()` functions and named `scale_color_*()`,
  `scale_colour_*()`, and `scale_fill_*()` helpers for every palette.
- Added automatic discrete, continuous, and binned scale selection.
- Added `gg_palette_info()`, `gg_palette_plot()`, and `gg_palette_check()` for
  catalogue discovery, previews, and palette diagnostics.
- Added alpha, direction, and Lab/RGB interpolation controls.
